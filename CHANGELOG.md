# Changelog

## 1.9.3 (22 Oct 2026)
- Dashboard graphs now use the Victory Farms report layout: green title with a rule, VF logo and "Victory Farms Confidential" footer, same as the slides.
- Slide and zip downloads: a visible "Preparing…" message and clear error text; if the photos cannot be loaded the slides are built without them; the zip always includes the pictures; the old report is cleared while a new one is built.

## 1.9.2 (22 Oct 2026)
- Slide deck: the overall-by-week line is drawn as its own continuous series, so it stays connected across weeks without data in PowerPoint, Google Slides and other viewers.

## 1.9.1 (22 Oct 2026)
- Slide deck: the overall rating by week is one connected line with a shaded area and the selected week enlarged, matching the PNG (no more broken line across weeks without data).

## 1.9.0 (22 Oct 2026)
- The "Log a check" tab is now **Audit**; the word "log" is removed from the app, reports, exports and file names (activity history, "Audited by", Audit CSV).
- Hand Washing Compliance and Foot Dipping Compliance (counted people) no longer need photo evidence: no photo warning, no photo-evidence slides, and they are left out of the photo-evidence percentages.
- Slides (.pptx) are now fully editable: real PowerPoint charts with their data behind them (right-click > Edit Data), editable tables, text and shapes on the Victory Farms layout. Photos remain pictures.

## 1.8.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Admin tab: Remove user (permanent). Past entries and activity stay under the person's username; you cannot remove yourself or the last active administrator.

## 1.7.1 (21 Oct 2026)
- Every checklist item is rated Good / Average / Bad. The six former Yes/No items no longer show Yes/No; older Yes/No answers still score the same and display as the matching band.

## 1.7.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Version number shown on the sign-in screen, in the header and in the Admin tab.
- Android/PWA manifest: app id, shortcuts (Log a check, Dashboard, Reports), portrait orientation.

## 1.6.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Phone / installed-app layout: bottom tab bar, compact Victory Farms header, larger touch controls.
- Victory Farms logo as the app icon and on the sign-in screen.

## 1.5.0 (21 Oct 2026)
- Dashboard graphs in Victory Farms colours.
- Report graphs and slides follow the Victory Farms PPT template, with a cover slide.
- Fixes: Yes/No rating list mismatch; low-memory photo upload on phones; faster loading.

## 1.4.0
- Admin: clear data for a period (with backup CSV). Export CSV by date range and location.

## 1.0.0 - 1.3.0
- Web app (installable), sign-in with roles, activity log, Excel import, reports.
